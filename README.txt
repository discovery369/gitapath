GitaPath – Telugu Translation Batch v21

This package continues from v20 and revises the Telugu translations for Bhagavad-gita 18.1–18.28. The Sanskrit text and transliteration are retained from the source edition. Verse 18.2 has been corrected to distinguish renunciation of desire-driven actions (sannyasa) from relinquishing attachment to the fruits of all actions (tyaga).

Coverage note: this batch revises chapter 18, verses 1–28 only. Telugu drafts are machine-assisted and should be reviewed by a fluent Telugu reader and a Sanskrit/Gita scholar before publication as authoritative translations.

Install: replace index.html and sw.js in the GitHub Pages repository root, commit to main, then open the app online once to refresh the service worker.
